# EarnOra Overview, Tasks & First-Hand Reviews

## What is EarnOra?
EarnOra (**Review. Earn. Grow.**) is a Kenyan consumer feedback and micro-task platform where verified members earn KES rewards and points by submitting honest, first-hand reviews and completing sponsored tasks across Kenya. Rewards are paid out in Kenyan Shillings (KES) via Safaricom M-Pesa B2C.

## Task & Review Categories
EarnOra organizes tasks into five main categories on the `/earn` and `/dashboard` pages:
1. **Restaurants (`hotels_restaurants`)**: Food, drinks, cafes, and hospitality dining reviews.
2. **Apps (`apps_websites`)**: Mobile application testing, web-app usability feedback, and feature evaluations.
3. **Local Business (`local_services`)**: Supermarkets, retail shops, salons, clinics, and neighborhood services.
4. **Telcos (`telco_fintech`)**: Mobile networks, data bundles, fiber internet, and mobile money apps.
5. **Products (`product_testing`)**: Consumer packaged goods, electronics, and household products.

## How to Submit a Review or Task (`/earn/submit`)
1. Browse available tasks on `/dashboard` or `/earn`. Sponsored tasks from verified businesses display a **"Sponsored"** badge.
2. Click on a task to open the submission page (`/earn/submit?task=<id>`).
3. Read the **Instructions & Proof Requirements** carefully (minimum word count, whether a receipt or screenshot photo is required).
4. Provide:
   - Star rating (1 to 5 stars) — honest ratings are required; low ratings are never penalized if the review is genuine.
   - Headline summary
   - Detailed first-hand review meeting the minimum word requirement (typically 30–50 words)
   - Pros and Cons
   - Optional or required photo/screenshot/receipt upload (JPEG, PNG, WEBP, or PDF up to 5 MB).
5. Submit your proof. The task reward initially enters your **Pending Earnings** while moderation verifies your submission.

## Approval Times & Moderation
- **Standard Review Time**: Most task and review submissions are moderated within **2 to 24 hours**.
- **Pending vs. Available Balance**:
  - **Pending Balance**: Rewards for submitted tasks currently awaiting verification.
  - **Available Balance**: Verified earnings ready for immediate M-Pesa withdrawal (minimum KES 100).
- **Why a Submission Might Be Rejected**:
  - Plagiarized, repetitive, or AI-generated filler text that does not reflect a genuine experience.
  - Missing or unrelated receipt/screenshot proof when required by the task instructions.
  - Duplicate submission (only one submission per user per campaign is permitted).
  - Self-reviewing a business you own.

## Fair Earnings Disclaimer
EarnOra rewards genuine consumer feedback and task completion based on available campaigns. Task availability varies by location and advertiser demand; EarnOra never promises guaranteed fixed income.
