# Account Settings, Theme, Security & Troubleshooting FAQs

## Switching Theme (Light / Dark / System)
- Click your **Profile avatar pill** in the top-right header to open the profile dropdown.
- Between **Account Settings** and **Sign Out**, use the **Theme** selector (`Light`, `Dark`, or `System`) to switch appearance immediately without closing the menu.
- You can also manage appearance, notifications, and data-saver preferences on `/settings`.

## Phone Verification & OTP Troubleshooting
- **Why Phone Verification is Required**: A verified Kenyan mobile number (`+2547...` or `+2541...`) is required before initiating M-Pesa B2C withdrawals.
- **OTP Expiry & Cooldown**: Verification codes are 6 digits, expire after 10 minutes, allow up to 5 attempts, and have a 60-second resend cooldown.
- **Changing Your Payout Number**: Visit `/settings` to view your profile and security settings.

## Security Tips
- **EarnOra Support will NEVER ask for your M-Pesa PIN, account password, or OTP code.**
- Never share screenshots containing your personal M-Pesa balance or unrelated private banking messages when uploading task receipts.
- Each reviewer may maintain only one personal EarnOra account. Multi-accounting or uploading duplicate receipt photos across accounts leads to suspension.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
1. **Why is my withdrawal failing?**
   - Check that your Available Balance is at least **KES 100**.
   - Check that your phone number is **M-Pesa Verified**.
   - Check that the amount does not exceed your current tier's **Max Per Withdrawal** or **Daily Withdrawal Limit** (shown on `/wallet` and the Dashboard **Wallet & Points** card).
2. **How do I increase my daily withdrawal limit?**
   - Earn points by completing reviews (+15 pts), sponsored tasks (+10 pts), watching sponsored ads (+2 pts), and maintaining your daily login streak. Reaching **Bronze (150 pts)**, **Silver (500 pts)**, **Gold (1,500 pts)**, or **Platinum (5,000 pts)** automatically raises your limits.
3. **Why does `/earn/ads` say "No ads available right now"?**
   - Sponsored ads come from active, funded advertiser campaigns. When all current spotlights have been watched or campaigns finish their budget, check back soon or complete review tasks on `/earn`.
4. **How do I contact a human support agent?**
   - Click **"Talk to a human / email support"** inside the AI Support Assistant to submit a support ticket with your conversation attached, or visit `/contact` / email `support@earnora.co.ke`.
