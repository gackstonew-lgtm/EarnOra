# EarnOra Points, Tiers & Streaks Guide

## How the Points & Tier System Works
Every verified action on EarnOra awards **Points** alongside KES rewards. Your total points determine your **Tier Level**, which directly raises your **daily and per-transaction M-Pesa withdrawal limits**.

## How You Earn Points
Points are awarded automatically on the server when actions are verified:
- **Approved First-Hand Review**: **+15 points**
- **Approved Sponsored Task**: **+10 points**
- **Watched Sponsored Ad (`/earn/ads`)**: **+2 points** (plus instant KES reward)
- **Daily Login Check-In**: **+1 point** per day
- **3-Day Streak Milestone Bonus**: **+5 points**
- **7-Day Streak Milestone Bonus**: **+15 points**
- **30-Day Streak Milestone Bonus**: **+50 points**

## Daily Caps & Fair Play Rules
- Every point source has a daily anti-abuse cap (for example: up to 10 watched ads per day, 1 daily login bonus per day, up to 10 approved tasks/reviews per day).
- **Idempotency**: The same task, review, ad, or daily check-in never awards points twice.
- **Revocation on Rejection**: If a submission is later rejected or flagged for violating review authenticity rules, any points awarded for that submission are automatically deducted.

## Tier Levels & M-Pesa Withdrawal Limits
There are five tiers on EarnOra:
1. **Starter Tier (0 – 149 pts)**:
   - Max per withdrawal: **KES 1,500**
   - Daily withdrawal limit: **KES 2,500 / day**
2. **Bronze Tier (150 – 499 pts)**:
   - Max per withdrawal: **KES 3,000**
   - Daily withdrawal limit: **KES 5,000 / day**
3. **Silver Tier (500 – 1,499 pts)**:
   - Max per withdrawal: **KES 7,500**
   - Daily withdrawal limit: **KES 15,000 / day**
4. **Gold Tier (1,500 – 4,999 pts)**:
   - Max per withdrawal: **KES 15,000**
   - Daily withdrawal limit: **KES 35,000 / day**
5. **Platinum Tier (5,000+ pts)**:
   - Max per withdrawal: **KES 50,000**
   - Daily withdrawal limit: **KES 100,000 / day**

## Daily Streaks
- Your **Streak** tracks consecutive days of active participation on EarnOra.
- Logging in daily and completing tasks keeps your streak alive and unlocks milestone point bonuses at 3, 7, and 30 days.
