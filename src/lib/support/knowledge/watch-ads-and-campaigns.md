# Watch Ads (`/earn/ads`) & Advertiser Campaign Portal

## Watch Ads & Earn (`/earn/ads`)
The **"Watch Ads. Earn Rewards."** feature lets you watch short sponsored brand spotlights (typically 15 seconds) from verified Kenyan businesses to earn instant KES rewards and **+2 Points** per ad.

### Rules for Watching Sponsored Ads
1. **Minimum Watch Duration**: Every ad has a server-verified countdown timer (e.g., 15 seconds). You must keep the spotlight open until the timer reaches 0s before claiming.
2. **One Reward Per Ad Per User**: Each unique sponsored ad can be rewarded once per user account.
3. **Daily Ad Limit**: You can watch and earn from up to **10 sponsored ads per day**. Your progress is shown on the dashboard banner (`"X of 10 ads watched today"`) and on `/earn/ads`.
4. **Cooldown**: There is a short **15-second cooldown** between completed ad watches to prevent automated abuse.
5. **Sponsored Transparency**: Every ad on EarnOra is clearly labeled **"Sponsored"**.

## Advertiser Portal & Campaign Wallet (`/advertiser`)
Businesses and brands in Kenya can sponsor tasks, product tests, and reviews through the **Advertiser Portal** (`/advertiser` or `/for-businesses`):
- **Advertiser Sign-Up**: Businesses register at `/advertiser/signup` with their business name, contact person, phone, email, and category.
- **Campaign Escrow Wallet (`/advertiser/wallet`)**:
  - Advertisers top up their wallet in KES via **M-Pesa Daraja STK Push** or **Card / Bank (Paystack / Flutterwave)**.
  - Every top-up generates a downloadable invoice/receipt.
- **Launching a Campaign (`/advertiser/campaigns/new`)**:
  - Total campaign cost = `(Reward per Task × Max Tasks) + 15% Platform Fee`.
  - Campaign funds are held in **atomic escrow** when the campaign is created so reviewers are guaranteed payout upon approval.
  - When a campaign ends or is stopped early, any **unused task reward budget** is automatically refunded to the advertiser's wallet.
- **Honest Review Policy (`/ad-review-policy`)**: Advertisers can never pay to remove low star ratings or demand 5-star praise. Submissions are judged strictly on proof authenticity and adherence to instructions.
