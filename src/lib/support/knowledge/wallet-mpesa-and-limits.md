# Wallet, M-Pesa Withdrawals, Limits & Fees

## Available vs. Pending Balance
- **Available Balance**: Confirmed KES funds in your EarnOra wallet that can be withdrawn immediately to your verified M-Pesa phone number.
- **Pending Balance**: KES rewards from submitted reviews or tasks that are currently in the moderation queue. Once approved, they move automatically to your Available Balance.
- **Watched Ad Rewards**: Completing a sponsored ad watch on `/earn/ads` credits your Available Balance and Points immediately upon server timestamp verification.

## How to Withdraw to M-Pesa (`/wallet`)
1. Open the **Withdraw / Wallet** page (`/wallet`).
2. Ensure your registered Kenyan phone number (`+2547...` or `+2541...`) shows **"Verified Line"**. If unverified, click **Verify Phone Number Now** to complete SMS OTP verification.
3. Enter the withdrawal amount in KES (or tap a quick preset chip: KES 100, KES 200, KES 500, KES 1,000).
4. Click **Confirm M-Pesa Withdrawal**.
5. Disbursements are processed via **Safaricom Daraja B2C** directly to your M-Pesa line, typically arriving within **60 seconds**.

## Withdrawal Rules & Limits
- **Minimum Withdrawal**: **KES 100** per transaction.
- **Tier-Based Limits**:
  - **Starter (0+ pts)**: Up to KES 1,500 per transaction, KES 2,500 per day.
  - **Bronze (150+ pts)**: Up to KES 3,000 per transaction, KES 5,000 per day.
  - **Silver (500+ pts)**: Up to KES 7,500 per transaction, KES 15,000 per day.
  - **Gold (1,500+ pts)**: Up to KES 15,000 per transaction, KES 35,000 per day.
  - **Platinum (5,000+ pts)**: Up to KES 50,000 per transaction, KES 100,000 per day.
- If you hit your per-transaction or daily withdrawal limit, you will see a message showing which tier unlocks the higher limit (for example: *"Reach Silver (500 pts) to withdraw up to KES 15,000 per day"*).

## Payout Fees
- Reviewer M-Pesa withdrawals on EarnOra have **no hidden platform withdrawal fee** deducted from your approved task reward. Platform fees (15%) are paid upfront by advertisers when funding campaigns.
